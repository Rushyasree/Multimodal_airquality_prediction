## 1. Executive Design Vision

To move away from plain white slides with dense, bullet-heavy layouts, the VayuDrishti presentation was completely redesigned from the ground up into a **modern AI and environmental intelligence presentation**. 

The design combines the aesthetic of an AI startup and research lab with the rigor expected of a B.Tech CSE capstone evaluation at VIT-AP University.

### Core Visual Principles
1. **Atmospheric AI + Environmental Theme:** Deep navy/slate canvas (`#0B132B`, `#0F172A`) paired with electric cyan (`#38BDF8`), teal (`#2DD4BF`), and emerald (`#10B981`) accents.
2. **Elevated Dark Glass Surfaces:** Translucent, rounded cards (`#162032`, `#1E293B`) with 1pt structural borders (`#334155`) and distinct colored top accent stripes.
3. **Diverse, Purpose-Built Layouts:** Every slide has a unique layout tailored to its technical content (hero slides, 2-phase roadmap, 3-mode connected pipelines, 2x2 problem grids, 4-step horizontal chevrons, comparison matrices, parity plots, and split-screen UI mockups).
4. **Classroom Projection Readability:** Large, crisp titles (22–25pt bold Segoe UI), bold white headings, high-contrast ice blue body text (`#E2E8F0`), and prominent numeric stat callouts (23pt electric cyan).
5. **Absolute Content & Scientific Integrity:** All experimental metrics ($R^2=0.949$, MAE 8.94 vs 14.82, live test $p=0.48/0.62$, $[-25, +25]\ \mu\text{g/m}^3$ vision limit, 45 km cutoff) and simplified student-friendly explanations are 100% preserved.
